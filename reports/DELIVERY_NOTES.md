This package is the **GitHub publication snapshot** requested after closure of B1–B4. It is not another training proposal. The executable integration notebook and every available distinct historical source are included; saved raw runtime data, secrets, and bulky per-machine outputs are not included.

## Recommended evaluator sequence

1. Read `README.md`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/RELEASE_STATUS.md`.
2. Review main `notebooks/00_MASTER_Integration.ipynb` and canonical `notebooks/core/01_Unified_v3_Phases_1_to_4.ipynb`.
3. Read `reports/PHASE_B_FINDINGS.md` and `results/reported/` to understand why the MLP was selected.
4. Open experiments only if auditing protocol details; archives are not independent final deliverables.
5. Cross-check scientific limits in `docs/PRIVACY_AND_PUBLICATION.md` and unfinished course tasks in `docs/ORIGINAL_REQUIREMENTS_CHECKLIST.md`.
